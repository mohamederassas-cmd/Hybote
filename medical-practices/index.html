The situation

Reception is with a patient.
The phone rings anyway.

Every unanswered call is a patient who calls the next practice on the list. Most of them do not call back.

01

The morning peak

Between 08:00 and 10:00 the phone rings far more often than one person can answer. Callers hear an engaged tone, hang up and try elsewhere.

02

Routine questions block the line

Opening hours, what to bring, is the referral still needed, has the result arrived. Necessary questions, but they occupy the line that an urgent case needs.

03

Appointments that nobody attends

An appointment booked weeks ago without a reminder is frequently missed. The slot stays empty while someone else waited for it.

How the figure is derived

Of 600 monthly calls, roughly 150 go unanswered. If 40 percent of those would have become an appointment, that is 60 treatments a month. At €180 average value this comes to about €10,800 a month, or roughly €130,000 a year.

In practice

What HYBOTE handles
at the front desk.

Administrative relief, with a clear boundary: HYBOTE organises, it does not advise medically.

Every call answered

No engaged tone, no queue. Calls are taken during the morning peak, at lunchtime, in the evening and at weekends, in the patient’s language.

Appointments booked and rescheduled

Working directly against your practice calendar, respecting appointment types, durations and which practitioner is responsible. Cancellations free the slot again immediately.

Reminders that reduce no shows

An automatic reminder before the appointment, with the option to cancel or move it in one message. Freed slots can be offered to someone on the waiting list.

Routine questions off the line

Opening hours, directions, what to bring, insurance and referral questions, repeat prescription requests. Answered from your own information, not invented.

Clear escalation for urgent cases

HYBOTE gives no medical advice. Anything that sounds urgent is passed to your team immediately or directed to the emergency number, following rules you define with us.

Documented for the team

Every call leaves a short, readable note: who called, what for, what was arranged. Your team starts the day with an overview instead of a stack of callback slips.
