About

Five-star service.
In every industry.

For over a decade, I worked in upscale and renowned hotels. There, nobody waits for an answer. You notice what a guest needs before they ask, and you reply in their language and in the right tone, at any hour.

That standard is not a technical skill. It is trained every day, with discretion as a given. My enthusiasm for AI came later, and with it one question: why should this level of service exist only at a hotel front desk?

HYBOTE is my answer. We bring the discipline of five-star hospitality into AI agents that answer every call and every message, 24/7, in over 50 languages. For everyone.
