About

Five-star service.
In every industry.

For over a decade, I worked in upscale and renowned hotels. There, nobody waits for an answer. You notice what a guest needs before they ask, and you reply in their language and in the right tone, at any hour.

That standard is not a technical skill. It is trained every day, with discretion as a given. My enthusiasm for AI came later, and with it one question: why should this level of service exist only at a hotel front desk?

HYBOTE is my answer. We bring the discipline of five-star hospitality into AI agents that answer every call and every message, 24/7, in over 50 languages. For everyone.

What hospitality taught me:

Nobody waits
In a luxury hotel, no guest waits for an answer. With HYBOTE, neither do your customers, at 3 PM or at 3 AM.
Every guest in their language
Guests arrive from all over the world and are answered in their language and tone. HYBOTE speaks over 50 languages.
Discretion as standard
What a guest shares stays protected. Your customer data is kept separate and never shared.
Knowing when a person takes over
A good concierge knows the limits of the role. Our agents hand over to your team with a short summary as soon as a person is needed.
