What it means for any business

  • Customers do not wait for office hours. The first business that answers with a clear next step usually gets the appointment.
  • The channel matters, but speed decides. Even on WhatsApp the typical answer arrived many hours later.
  • Silence is the most common answer. More than half of the enquiries in this test never got one.

Test your own business in ten minutes

  1. Ask someone outside your team to send one enquiry by WhatsApp and one by email after 6 PM, from a number you do not know.
  2. Write down the exact time of the first reply on each channel, or note that none came.
  3. Check whether the reply answered the question and asked one question back, such as budget or timing.
  4. Compare your times with this benchmark: 5 minutes, one hour and the 9.4 hour median.

How the test worked

  • HYBOTE sent anonymous buyer enquiries to real estate brokerages in the GCC between 13 July and 10 September 2026, by WhatsApp (57) and email (87).
  • Only the first valid test per brokerage and channel counts, so no company is counted twice.
  • We measured the time to the first reply within the measurement window. No reply within the window counts as no reply.
  • All results are aggregated. No brokerage, agent or person is named or described, and any group below 20 enquiries is left out.
  • Percentages are rounded to whole numbers.
